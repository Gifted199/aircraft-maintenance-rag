# A-200 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The A-200 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 61-10-02: Propeller Assembly - Installation

- **Aircraft type:** A-200
- **ATA chapter:** 61 - Propellers
- **Task number:** 61-10-02
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2024-12-02
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 20 to 200 Nm
- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Propeller sling
- Hoist, 250 kg minimum
- Engine oil

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A200-61-2203 | O-ring, propeller shaft flange | 1 | 3 |
| A200-61-2210 | Nut, propeller mounting | 8 | 5 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Propeller mounting nuts, first stage | 60 Nm | 6 |
| Propeller mounting nuts, final torque | 115 Nm | 6 |
| Spinner screws | 35 in-lb | 7 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE ENGINE CANNOT BE STARTED WHILE YOU WORK ON THE PROPELLER. A PROPELLER THAT TURNS CAN KILL OR INJURE PERSONNEL.

Open the circuit breakers IGNITION and START CTL for the applicable engine and attach DO NOT CLOSE tags.

#### Step 2

Clean the propeller shaft flange and the mounting face of the propeller hub. Examine them for damage.

#### Step 3

Lubricate a new O-ring (P/N A200-61-2203) with engine oil and install it in the groove of the propeller shaft flange.

#### Step 4

> **CAUTION:** Keep the propeller supported by the sling at all times. Do not let the blades touch the ground or the work stand.

Lift the propeller assembly with the sling and hoist and align it with the dowels of the propeller shaft flange.

#### Step 5

Install the 8 mounting nuts (P/N A200-61-2210) with their washers and tighten them by hand.

#### Step 6

> **WARNING:** DO NOT OPERATE THE ENGINE UNTIL ALL 8 MOUNTING NUTS ARE AT THE FINAL TORQUE. A PROPELLER THAT IS NOT CORRECTLY ATTACHED CAN SEPARATE FROM THE ENGINE.

Torque the mounting nuts in a cross pattern in two stages: first to 60 Nm, then to the final torque of 115 Nm.

#### Step 7

Install the spinner and torque the spinner screws to 35 in-lb.

#### Step 8

Remove the sling, remove the tags and close the circuit breakers. Do an engine ground run and make sure there are no oil leaks at the propeller shaft flange and no unusual vibration.
