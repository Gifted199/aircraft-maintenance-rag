## TASK 28-21-05: Fuel Boost Pump - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** A-200
- **ATA chapter:** 28 - Fuel
- **Task number:** 28-21-05
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2025-02-17
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Bonding meter
- Grounding cables
- Fuel-resistant container
- Defuelling equipment

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A200-28-2108 | O-ring, fuel boost pump | 1 | 6 |
| A200-28-2105 | Fuel boost pump | 1 | 6 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel boost pump mounting bolts | 50 in-lb | 7 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** FUEL VAPOUR IS FLAMMABLE AND EXPLOSIVE. DO NOT PERMIT OPEN FLAMES, SPARKS OR SMOKING WITHIN 15 METRES OF THE AIRCRAFT. BOND THE AIRCRAFT AND ALL EQUIPMENT TO GROUND BEFORE YOU OPEN THE FUEL SYSTEM.

Connect the aircraft and the defuelling equipment to an approved ground point.

#### Step 2

Open the circuit breaker L FUEL BOOST PUMP and attach a DO NOT CLOSE tag.

#### Step 3

Defuel the left wing collector tank.

#### Step 4

> **CAUTION:** Put a container below the pump. Some fuel will drain when you remove the pump.

Remove access panel 521AB and disconnect the electrical connector from the fuel boost pump.

#### Step 5

Remove the 6 mounting bolts and remove the fuel boost pump. Discard the O-ring.

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Use only clean fuel to lubricate the O-ring. Grease or oil will contaminate the fuel system.

Lubricate a new O-ring (P/N A200-28-2108) with clean fuel and install it on the new fuel boost pump (P/N A200-28-2105).

#### Step 7

Install the fuel boost pump with the 6 mounting bolts and torque the bolts to 50 in-lb.

#### Step 8

Connect the electrical connector. Measure the bonding resistance between the pump body and the structure. The resistance must not be more than 0.010 ohm.

#### Step 9

Refuel the left wing collector tank, remove the tag and close the circuit breaker L FUEL BOOST PUMP.

#### Step 10

> **WARNING:** DO NOT OPERATE THE FUEL BOOST PUMP IN A DRY TANK. THE PUMP CAN OVERHEAT AND IGNITE FUEL VAPOUR.

Operate the fuel boost pump. Make sure the fuel pressure is between 22 and 28 psi and that there are no leaks at the pump.

#### Step 11

Install access panel 521AB.
