## TASK 32-42-03: Main Wheel Brake Assembly - Wear Check and Replacement

- **Aircraft type:** B-340
- **ATA chapter:** 32 - Landing Gear
- **Task number:** 32-42-03
- **Revision:** 3
- **Revision date:** 2026-03-02
- **Supersedes:** Revision 2

### Revision summary

General revision of the task. No earlier revisions of this task are held in this library.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 Nm
- Depth gauge or steel rule
- Wheel jack
- Brake bleed equipment
- Protective caps and plugs

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| B340-32-5301 | Brake assembly, main wheel | 1 | 7 |
| B340-32-5306 | Bolt, brake attachment | 8 | 7 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Brake attachment bolts | 75 Nm | 7 |
| Brake hydraulic line fitting | 27 Nm | 8 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** DO NOT TOUCH THE BRAKE ASSEMBLY UNTIL IT IS COOL. HOT BRAKES CAN CAUSE SEVERE BURNS.

Chock the nose wheels, set the parking brake and let the brakes cool.

#### Step 2

With the parking brake set, measure how far each wear pin extends from the brake housing. Replace the brake assembly if any wear pin extends less than 3.0 mm. If all wear pins are in limits, record the measurements and stop this task here.

#### Step 3

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE HYDRAULIC PRESSURE IS ZERO BEFORE YOU DISCONNECT THE BRAKE LINE. HYDRAULIC FLUID UNDER PRESSURE CAN PENETRATE THE SKIN.

Release the parking brake and depressurise the hydraulic system.

#### Step 4

Lift the main wheel on a jack and remove the main wheel.

#### Step 5

> **CAUTION:** Hydraulic fluid damages paint and tyres. Clean spilled fluid immediately.

Disconnect the brake hydraulic line. Install caps on the line and on the brake port.

#### Step 6

Remove the 8 brake attachment bolts and remove the brake assembly from the axle flange.

#### Step 7

> **CAUTION:** Do not use the removed attachment bolts again. Always install new bolts.

Install the new brake assembly (P/N B340-32-5301) with 8 new attachment bolts (P/N B340-32-5306). Torque the bolts in a cross pattern to 75 Nm.

#### Step 8

Connect the brake hydraulic line and torque the fitting to 27 Nm.

#### Step 9

Bleed the brake system, then install the main wheel and lower the aircraft.

#### Step 10

Pressurise the hydraulic system and apply the brakes 5 times. Make sure there are no leaks, then measure and record the wear pin length of the new brake assembly.
