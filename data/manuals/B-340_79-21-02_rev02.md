## TASK 79-21-02: Engine Oil Filter Element - Replacement

- **Aircraft type:** B-340
- **ATA chapter:** 79 - Engine Oil
- **Task number:** 79-21-02
- **Revision:** 2
- **Revision date:** 2025-12-15
- **Supersedes:** Revision 1

### Revision summary

General revision of the task. No earlier revisions of this task are held in this library.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 5 to 50 Nm
- Container, 5 litres
- Lockwire, 0.8 mm
- Engine oil

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| B340-79-2102 | Filter element, engine oil | 1 | 5 |
| B340-79-2105 | O-ring, filter cover | 1 | 5 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil filter cover | 16 Nm | 6 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** HOT ENGINE OIL CAN CAUSE SEVERE BURNS. LET THE ENGINE COOL BEFORE YOU OPEN THE OIL SYSTEM.

Make sure the engine has been shut down for a minimum of 15 minutes.

#### Step 2

Open the engine cowl and put a container below the oil filter housing.

#### Step 3

Cut the lockwire, remove the filter cover and remove the oil filter element. Discard the O-ring.

#### Step 4

> **CAUTION:** If you find metal particles in the filter element, do not release the engine for service. Keep the element for laboratory analysis.

Examine the removed filter element for metal particles.

#### Step 5

Install the new oil filter element (P/N B340-79-2102) and a new O-ring (P/N B340-79-2105) lubricated with clean engine oil.

#### Step 6

Install the filter cover and torque it to 16 Nm. Safety the cover with lockwire.

#### Step 7

Check the engine oil level and add oil if necessary. Close the engine cowl.

#### Step 8

> **CAUTION:** Do not increase power above idle until the oil pressure is stable in the green range.

Do an engine ground run at idle for 5 minutes, shut down the engine and make sure there are no leaks at the filter cover.
