# C-75 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The C-75 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 32-21-02: Nose Landing Gear Shimmy Damper - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** C-75
- **ATA chapter:** 32 - Landing Gear
- **Task number:** 32-21-02
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2025-03-24
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 5 to 25 Nm
- Nose jack
- Wheel chocks
- Cotter pin pliers

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| C75-32-6202 | Shimmy damper, nose landing gear | 1 | 4 |
| C75-32-6209 | Cotter pin, piston rod nut | 1 | 6 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Shimmy damper body attachment bolt | 14 Nm | 5 |
| Shimmy damper piston rod nut | 9 Nm | 6 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE AIRCRAFT IS STABLE ON THE JACK AND THE MAIN WHEELS ARE CHOCKED. AN AIRCRAFT THAT FALLS FROM THE JACK CAN KILL OR INJURE PERSONNEL.

Lift the nose of the aircraft on a jack until the nose wheel is clear of the ground.

#### Step 2

Remove the cotter pin, the nut and the bolt that attach the damper piston rod to the steering collar. Discard the cotter pin.

#### Step 3

Remove the bolt that attaches the damper body to the nose gear leg and remove the shimmy damper.

#### Step 4

Before installation, move the piston rod of the new shimmy damper (P/N C75-32-6202) through its full stroke by hand. The resistance must be smooth and constant and there must be no fluid leaks.

#### Step 5

Attach the damper body to the nose gear leg with the body attachment bolt and torque the bolt to 14 Nm.

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Do not loosen the nut to align the cotter pin hole. Do not use the removed cotter pin again.

Attach the piston rod to the steering collar and torque the nut to 9 Nm. Tighten the nut to the next slot and install a new cotter pin (P/N C75-32-6209).

#### Step 7

Turn the nose wheel through its full steering range in both directions. Make sure there is no binding.

#### Step 8

Lower the aircraft from the jack.
