# A-200 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The A-200 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 21-31-04: Cabin Pressure Sensor - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** A-200
- **ATA chapter:** 21 - Air Conditioning and Pressurisation
- **Task number:** 21-31-04
- **Revision:** 2
- **Revision date:** 2025-11-03
- **Supersedes:** Revision 1

### Revision summary

The cabin pressure sensor part number is changed. The earlier sensor must not be installed.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Torque wrench, 5 to 25 Nm
- Protective caps and plugs
- Leak detection fluid
- Cabin pressurisation test unit

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A200-21-3115 | O-ring, sensor port | 1 | 7 |
| A200-21-3110 | Cabin pressure sensor | 1 | 7 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor mounting bolts | 35 in-lb | 8 |
| Sense line B-nut | 15 Nm | 9 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** DO NOT DISCONNECT THE PRESSURE SENSE LINE WHILE THE CABIN IS PRESSURISED. A RAPID RELEASE OF PRESSURE CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONNEL.

Make sure the aircraft is on the ground, the cabin is fully depressurised and the outflow valve is open.

#### Step 2

Open the circuit breaker CABIN PRESS CTL and attach a DO NOT CLOSE tag.

#### Step 3

Remove access panel 131AL to get access to the cabin pressure sensor on the aft pressure bulkhead.

#### Step 4

> **CAUTION:** Do not pull on the wiring harness. Hold the connector body when you disconnect it.

Disconnect the electrical connector from the sensor. Install protective caps on the connector and the receptacle.

#### Step 5

Disconnect the sense line B-nut from the sensor. Install a cap on the sense line.

#### Step 6

Remove the 4 mounting bolts and remove the sensor. Discard the O-ring.

#### Step 7

> **CAUTION:** Do not use sealant or thread tape on the sense port. Contamination will block the port and cause an incorrect cabin pressure indication.

Install a new O-ring (P/N A200-21-3115) on the new cabin pressure sensor (P/N A200-21-3110).

#### Step 8

Put the sensor in position, install the 4 mounting bolts and torque them to 35 in-lb.

#### Step 9

Connect the sense line to the sensor and torque the B-nut to 15 Nm.

#### Step 10

Connect the electrical connector. Remove the tag and close the circuit breaker CABIN PRESS CTL.

#### Step 11

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE ALL PERSONNEL ARE CLEAR OF THE DOORS AND HATCHES BEFORE YOU PRESSURISE THE CABIN.

Pressurise the cabin to 2.0 psi differential and do a leak check of the sense line connection with leak detection fluid. No bubbles are permitted.

#### Step 12

Do the functional test of the cabin pressure control system, then install access panel 131AL.
