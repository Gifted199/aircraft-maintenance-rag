# C-75 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The C-75 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 24-31-01: Main Battery - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** C-75
- **ATA chapter:** 24 - Electrical Power
- **Task number:** 24-31-01
- **Revision:** 2
- **Revision date:** 2026-03-23
- **Supersedes:** Revision 1

### Revision summary

The torque of the battery terminal bolts is changed. Use only the value given in this revision.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Torque wrench, 5 to 25 Nm
- Insulated spanners
- Eye protection and protective gloves
- Multimeter

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| C75-24-6101 | Main battery, 24 V | 1 | 7 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Battery hold-down nuts | 45 in-lb | 7 |
| Battery terminal bolts | 12 Nm | 8 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

Make sure external power is disconnected and the battery switch is in the OFF position.

#### Step 2

> **WARNING:** BATTERY ELECTROLYTE IS CORROSIVE. WEAR EYE PROTECTION AND PROTECTIVE GLOVES WHEN YOU WORK ON THE BATTERY.

Open the battery compartment door.

#### Step 3

> **WARNING:** ALWAYS DISCONNECT THE NEGATIVE CABLE FIRST. A TOOL THAT TOUCHES THE POSITIVE TERMINAL AND THE STRUCTURE AT THE SAME TIME CAN CAUSE AN ARC, BURNS AND FIRE.

Disconnect the negative (-) cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive (+) cable.

#### Step 4

Disconnect the vent tubes and the temperature sensor connector.

#### Step 5

> **CAUTION:** The battery weighs 26 kg. Two persons are necessary to lift it.

Loosen the hold-down nuts and remove the battery from the tray.

#### Step 6

Examine the battery tray for corrosion and spilled electrolyte. Clean the tray if necessary.

#### Step 7

Put the main battery (P/N C75-24-6101) on the tray and torque the hold-down nuts to 45 in-lb.

#### Step 8

> **CAUTION:** Do not torque the terminal bolts more than the specified value. This will damage the battery terminals.

Connect the positive (+) cable first, then the negative (-) cable. Torque the terminal bolts to 12 Nm.

#### Step 9

Connect the vent tubes and the temperature sensor connector.

#### Step 10

Set the battery switch to ON and make sure the battery voltage is not less than 24 V. Set the battery switch to OFF and close the battery compartment door.
