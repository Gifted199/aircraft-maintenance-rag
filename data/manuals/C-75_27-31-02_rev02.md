## TASK 27-31-02: Elevator Trim Actuator - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** C-75
- **ATA chapter:** 27 - Flight Controls
- **Task number:** 27-31-02
- **Revision:** 2
- **Revision date:** 2025-05-12
- **Supersedes:** Revision 1

### Revision summary

General revision of the task. No earlier revisions of this task are held in this library.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Inclinometer
- Cotter pin pliers

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| C75-27-6302 | Elevator trim actuator | 1 | 5 |
| C75-27-6307 | Bolt, trim actuator rod end | 1 | 6 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Trim actuator mounting bolts | 50 in-lb | 5 |
| Trim actuator rod-end nut | 30 in-lb | 6 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE ELECTRICAL POWER TO THE TRIM SYSTEM IS OFF. MOVEMENT OF THE TRIM TAB CAN INJURE PERSONNEL.

Set the elevator trim to neutral, open the circuit breaker PITCH TRIM and attach a DO NOT CLOSE tag.

#### Step 2

Remove access panel 331BB on the lower surface of the horizontal stabiliser.

#### Step 3

Disconnect the electrical connector from the trim actuator and remove the rod-end bolt at the trim tab horn.

#### Step 4

Remove the 4 mounting bolts and remove the elevator trim actuator.

#### Step 5

Install the new elevator trim actuator (P/N C75-27-6302) with the 4 mounting bolts and torque the bolts to 50 in-lb.

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Make sure the rod end can turn freely on the bolt after you torque the nut. A rod end that cannot turn will cause the actuator to bind.

Connect the rod end to the trim tab horn with a new rod-end bolt (P/N C75-27-6307). Torque the nut to 30 in-lb and install a new cotter pin.

#### Step 7

Connect the electrical connector, remove the tag and close the circuit breaker PITCH TRIM.

#### Step 8

> **WARNING:** A TRIM TAB THAT MOVES IN THE WRONG DIRECTION OR BEYOND ITS LIMITS CAN CAUSE LOSS OF CONTROL OF THE AIRCRAFT. DO NOT RELEASE THE AIRCRAFT UNTIL THE DIRECTION AND THE TRAVEL ARE CORRECT.

Operate the elevator trim through its full range. The trim tab travel must be 12 degrees up and 20 degrees down. Make sure a nose-up trim command moves the trim tab down.

#### Step 9

Get an independent inspection of the installation by a second authorised person, then install access panel 331BB.
