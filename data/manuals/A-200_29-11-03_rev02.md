## TASK 29-11-03: Hydraulic Return Filter Element - Replacement

- **Aircraft type:** A-200
- **ATA chapter:** 29 - Hydraulic Power
- **Task number:** 29-11-03
- **Revision:** 2
- **Revision date:** 2025-09-08
- **Supersedes:** Revision 1

### Revision summary

General revision of the task. No earlier revisions of this task are held in this library.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 5 to 50 Nm
- Fluid-resistant container
- Lockwire, 0.8 mm
- Hydraulic fluid
- Protective gloves

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A200-29-3303 | Filter element, hydraulic return | 1 | 5 |
| A200-29-3307 | O-ring, filter bowl | 1 | 5 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Hydraulic return filter bowl | 25 Nm | 6 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE HYDRAULIC PRESSURE IS ZERO BEFORE YOU OPEN THE FILTER. HYDRAULIC FLUID UNDER PRESSURE CAN PENETRATE THE SKIN.

Depressurise the hydraulic system and release the pressure in the hydraulic reservoir.

#### Step 2

> **CAUTION:** Hydraulic fluid damages paint and irritates the skin. Wear protective gloves and clean spilled fluid immediately.

Put a container below the return filter, cut the lockwire and remove the filter bowl.

#### Step 3

Remove the filter element and the O-ring from the filter bowl and discard them.

#### Step 4

Examine the filter bowl and the removed element for metal particles. If you find metal particles, do not continue; report the contamination for investigation.

#### Step 5

Install the new filter element (P/N A200-29-3303) and a new O-ring (P/N A200-29-3307) lubricated with clean hydraulic fluid.

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Do not torque the filter bowl more than the specified value. This will damage the O-ring and cause a leak.

Install the filter bowl and torque it to 25 Nm. Safety the bowl with lockwire.

#### Step 7

Fill the hydraulic reservoir to the correct level, pressurise the hydraulic system and make sure there are no leaks at the filter bowl. Make sure the differential pressure indicator is reset.
