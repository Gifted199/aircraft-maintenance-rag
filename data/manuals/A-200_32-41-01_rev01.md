# A-200 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The A-200 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 32-41-01: Main Wheel Axle Nut - Installation and Torque

- **Aircraft type:** A-200
- **ATA chapter:** 32 - Landing Gear
- **Task number:** 32-41-01
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2024-02-12
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 20 to 250 Nm
- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Axle nut socket
- Wheel bearing grease
- Lockwire, 0.8 mm

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| A200-32-4107 | Axle nut, main wheel | 1 | 2 |
| A200-32-4109 | Lock bolt, axle nut | 2 | 9 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Axle nut, bearing seating torque | 200 Nm | 5 |
| Axle nut, final torque | 120 Nm | 7 |
| Axle nut lock bolts | 45 in-lb | 9 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE AIRCRAFT IS STABLE ON THE JACKS AND THE AREA BELOW THE LANDING GEAR IS CLEAR. AN AIRCRAFT THAT FALLS FROM THE JACKS CAN KILL OR INJURE PERSONNEL.

Make sure the aircraft is on jacks and the main wheel is clear of the ground.

#### Step 2

> **CAUTION:** Do not use a wire brush on the axle threads. Damage to the protective coating will cause corrosion.

Examine the axle threads and the axle nut (P/N A200-32-4107) for damage and corrosion. Replace the axle nut if the threads are damaged.

#### Step 3

Apply a thin layer of wheel bearing grease to the axle threads and to the bearing face of the axle nut.

#### Step 4

Install the axle nut by hand until it touches the outer wheel bearing.

#### Step 5

> **CAUTION:** Turn the wheel continuously while you apply the seating torque. If the wheel does not turn, the bearings will not seat correctly.

While you turn the wheel by hand, torque the axle nut to 200 Nm to seat the bearings.

#### Step 6

Loosen the axle nut until the torque is zero.

#### Step 7

> **WARNING:** DO NOT USE THE SEATING TORQUE AS THE FINAL TORQUE. AN AXLE NUT WITH TOO MUCH TORQUE CAN CAUSE BEARING FAILURE AND LOSS OF THE WHEEL.

While you turn the wheel by hand, torque the axle nut to the final torque of 120 Nm.

#### Step 8

> **CAUTION:** Do not loosen the axle nut to align the locking holes.

If the locking holes in the axle nut and the axle are not aligned, tighten the axle nut to the next hole.

#### Step 9

Install the 2 lock bolts (P/N A200-32-4109) and torque them to 45 in-lb. Safety the lock bolts with lockwire.

#### Step 10

Make sure the wheel turns freely and has no axial play. Install the hub cap and lower the aircraft from the jacks.
