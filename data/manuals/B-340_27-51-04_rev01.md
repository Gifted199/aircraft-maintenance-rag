## TASK 27-51-04: Flap Actuator - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** B-340
- **ATA chapter:** 27 - Flight Controls
- **Task number:** 27-51-04
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2025-01-13
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 Nm
- Flap safety locks
- Protective caps and plugs
- Cotter pin pliers

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| B340-27-7504 | Flap actuator | 1 | 5 |
| B340-27-7511 | Bolt, actuator body attachment | 1 | 5 |
| B340-27-7513 | Bolt, actuator rod end | 1 | 6 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Flap actuator body attachment bolt | 48 Nm | 5 |
| Flap actuator rod-end bolt | 34 Nm | 6 |
| Flap actuator hydraulic fittings | 20 Nm | 7 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** INSTALL THE FLAP SAFETY LOCKS BEFORE YOU WORK IN THE FLAP AREA. FLAP MOVEMENT CAN KILL OR INJURE PERSONNEL.

Extend the flaps fully and install the flap safety locks.

#### Step 2

Open the circuit breaker FLAP CTL, attach a DO NOT CLOSE tag and depressurise the hydraulic system.

#### Step 3

Disconnect the electrical connector of the position sensor and the two hydraulic lines from the flap actuator. Install caps on the lines and ports.

#### Step 4

> **CAUTION:** The flap actuator weighs 9 kg. Support it before you remove the last bolt.

Remove the rod-end bolt that attaches the actuator to the flap. Support the actuator and remove the body attachment bolt.

#### Step 5

Put the new flap actuator (P/N B340-27-7504) in position, install the body attachment bolt (P/N B340-27-7511) and torque it to 48 Nm.

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Do not use force to install the rod-end bolt. A bolt that does not go in freely shows that the rod end is not adjusted correctly.

Adjust the rod end until the rod-end bolt (P/N B340-27-7513) goes in freely by hand. Torque the rod-end bolt to 34 Nm and install a new cotter pin.

#### Step 7

Connect the two hydraulic lines and torque the fittings to 20 Nm. Connect the electrical connector.

#### Step 8

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE ALL PERSONNEL AND EQUIPMENT ARE CLEAR OF THE FLAPS BEFORE YOU PRESSURISE THE HYDRAULIC SYSTEM.

Remove the flap safety locks, remove the tag, close the circuit breaker FLAP CTL and pressurise the hydraulic system.

#### Step 9

Operate the flaps through 3 full cycles. Make sure the movement is smooth, there is no FLAP ASYMMETRY caution and there are no hydraulic leaks at the actuator.
