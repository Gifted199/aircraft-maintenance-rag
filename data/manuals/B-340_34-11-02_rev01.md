# B-340 Aircraft Maintenance Manual

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA - NOT FOR MAINTENANCE USE.** The B-340 is a fictional aircraft and all part numbers and values are invented.

## TASK 34-11-02: Pitot Probe - Removal and Installation

- **Aircraft type:** B-340
- **ATA chapter:** 34 - Navigation
- **Task number:** 34-11-02
- **Revision:** 1
- **Revision date:** 2024-09-30
- **Supersedes:** None (initial issue)

### Revision summary

Initial issue.

### Tools and equipment

- Torque wrench, 10 to 100 in-lb
- Torque wrench, 5 to 25 Nm
- Pitot-static test set
- Aerodynamic sealant
- Protective caps

### Parts and materials

| Part number | Description | Quantity | Used in step |
|---|---|---|---|
| B340-34-5105 | Gasket, pitot probe | 1 | 5 |
| B340-34-5102 | Pitot probe, heated | 1 | 5 |

### Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Applied in step |
|---|---|---|
| Pitot line B-nut | 14 Nm | 6 |
| Pitot probe mounting screws | 30 in-lb | 7 |

### Procedure

#### Step 1

> **WARNING:** MAKE SURE THE PITOT HEAT IS OFF AND THE PROBE IS COOL BEFORE YOU TOUCH IT. A HOT PITOT PROBE CAN CAUSE SEVERE BURNS.

Open the circuit breaker PITOT HEAT and attach a DO NOT CLOSE tag.

#### Step 2

> **CAUTION:** Do not pull the probe out more than necessary. This can damage the wiring harness and the pitot line.

Remove the 6 mounting screws and carefully pull the pitot probe out until you can get access to the connections.

#### Step 3

Disconnect the electrical connector and the pitot line from the probe. Install a cap on the pitot line.

#### Step 4

Remove the pitot probe and discard the gasket.

#### Step 5

Install a new gasket (P/N B340-34-5105) on the new pitot probe (P/N B340-34-5102).

#### Step 6

> **CAUTION:** Do not blow into the pitot probe or apply pressure to it unless the pitot-static test set is connected. This can damage the airspeed instruments.

Connect the pitot line and torque the B-nut to 14 Nm. Connect the electrical connector.

#### Step 7

Put the probe in position, install the 6 mounting screws and torque them to 30 in-lb.

#### Step 8

Apply aerodynamic sealant around the base of the probe and let it cure.

#### Step 9

Do a leak test of the pitot system with the pitot-static test set at 150 knots indicated. The leak rate must not be more than 1 knots in one minute.

#### Step 10

> **CAUTION:** Do not operate the pitot heat on the ground for more than 30 seconds. The probe will overheat.

Remove the tag, close the circuit breaker PITOT HEAT and do an operational test of the pitot heat.
